3 · Where you intervene

Phase 0 — set the targets You approve what "good" means: the metric targets the audit closes against.
Phase 4 — the decision gate Scope approval, keep/delete calls on findings, and anything that touches credentials, money, or data history. Everything mechanical proceeds without you.
Blocked paths, anytime Fixes that need your password, your account, or your authority queue up as a dry-run command list, never worked around.
Phase 8 — the close You read the before/after table and the red-team report, and accept or reopen.

Three rules that hold throughout

  • Read-only until Phase 4. Diagnosis changes nothing, so the findings stay honest.
  • Never fix a rule with another rule. If a rule was rewritten before and the problem persisted, the fix is mechanical or deletion.
  • Nothing is "done" without the verification run and read back in the same session. Changelog entries carry the command and its output.
